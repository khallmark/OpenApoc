Verdict

OpenApoc already extracts the bulk of the live rule tables from the ISO non-4 EXEs (research, vehicles, economy, orgs, TACP equipment). The remaining original-game work is not “find more blobs.” It is logic that the tables do not encode: 36 TPS, mission defaults, org vehicle purchases, cover AI, and hazard constants.

Steam depot is the wrong extract target. Depot UFO2P.EXE / TACP.EXE are the Pentium 4 builds (CRC 0xdbd3b41d / 0x3ec9c268). Extractors expect non-4 (0x4749ffc1 / 0xfebbe39e), log an error, and still apply non-4 offsets — garbage tables.
The tick lie is a 4× scalar. Original simulation is 36 TPS. OpenApoc sets TICKS_PER_SECOND = 144. Training, fire, enzyme, wounds, and AOE all inherit that until each subsystem is rebased.
32 hexa ranges have no extractor header. Highest-value city misses: UFO_mission_data, manufacturing_data, UFO_growth_rates, vehicle_park_spawn_table, senate_relationships. Some aequip/damage copies in UFO2P are TACP-owned in OpenApoc.
Original stubs exist too. selmode==7/8 is explicitly “Not implemented” in UFO2P. Designer/Editor, GRAB, Scenario Generator, and SPARES samples have no OpenApoc analogue — do not implement them.
