# Validation findings: final-verdict summary

**Summary.** One row for each of the 193 findings in `validation_register.md`, showing the verdict and impact after the 34 rulings in `adjudications.md` were applied. Rows are sorted by final impact (critical, high, medium, low), then validator key, then ID. Impact is the finding's weight for the Superhuman strategy (engine difficulty 4). The register has detail blocks only for its 117 critical/high/medium rows; the 76 low rows are summarised from their one-line title plus the per-mechanic write-ups in `docs/original-game/findings/validation-2026-10/v_*.md`. Totals: 193 findings, 58 match, 81 diverges, 25 engine_missing, 12 engine_bug, 16 unbound, 1 bound but not fully compared.

## How to read it

**Precedence.** `adjudications.md` supersedes `validation_register.md` for every adjudicated row (verdict, impact and tree come from the ruling). `resolved-facts.md` is a cross-check; where it differs, the ruling wins. The v_*.md write-ups number some findings differently from the register (v_gov_income G2/G7 are register G4/G8; v_base_defence F01, F04, F08, F14, F16 are BD01a/b, BD04, BD08, BD14a, BD16); this table uses register IDs only. Each rule difference is EXE first and historical engine snapshot second. Numbers are Superhuman unless a formula in D is shown.

**Final verdict.** `match` (same rule), `diverges` (both have the rule, parameters or structure differ), `engine_missing` (EXE rule absent from the engine), `engine_bug` (engine implements it wrongly or on a stale input), `exe_unbound` (EXE side unknown), `bound_partial` (F17 only: EXE writer found, engine formula only partly compared).

**Target tree (historical).** `both` means the October LIVE snapshot and HEAD at `fda36d6c`; `HEAD` means only that old committed base lacked the change; `none` means no change; `?` is unresolved. Non-adjudicated rows take the register's target. The former LIVE funding/base-trigger changes later entered local `879d0d73`, in the six-commit lineage ending `bce89743`. They need no snapshot commit. This provenance correction leaves historical verdicts intact; it does not identify old benchmark binaries or assert remote publication.

**Group (historical directional census, not the implementation order).** A relaxes harsher engine behaviour (40 rows); B adds pressure or removes excess income (49); C is mixed or fidelity-only (35); none matches (50); ? has unresolved direction (19). HEAD-only rows describe that old base, not LIVE. Findings sharing a fix share its group. The completed [04 engine plan](../04-engine-fidelity.md) owns sequencing, including A0/A-now/A-late and its explicit regroupings. Its fix groups are not a replacement verdict census.
